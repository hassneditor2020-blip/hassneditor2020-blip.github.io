FAQ

Before we start—quick answers

01How long does a video project take?+

Timing depends on the video length, complexity, available footage and required formats. I confirm a clear schedule after reviewing your brief.

02Do I need to have footage ready?+

No. I can edit your existing footage, arrange professional filming in Ajman, Sharjah or Dubai by appointment, or create suitable AI scenes depending on the project.

03Which video formats do you deliver?+

I prepare vertical 9:16, horizontal 16:9, square 1:1 and other platform-specific formats as required.

04How are revisions handled?+

The review process and required revisions are agreed clearly before production so feedback and delivery remain organized.

05Can we work together outside the UAE?+

Yes. Filming is available in Ajman, Sharjah and Dubai by appointment. Editing and motion graphics are available remotely in the UAE and worldwide.

06How is the project cost calculated?+

Cost depends on the editing style, scope, duration, supplied materials, motion graphics, AI scenes, revisions and delivery timing. Send the project brief for an accurate custom quote.
