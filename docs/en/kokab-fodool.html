MY ROLE / AI-ASSISTED PRODUCTION

From a question to a finished episode

AI tools support production. The creative direction connects the concept, character, motion, Arabic dialogue and final edit.

  1. 01

    Concept & dialogue

    Define a learning question or goal and shape the dialogue for the episode and audience.

  2. 02

    Character & scene direction

    Visual references, motion and camera direction, with character identity and continuity review.

  3. 03

    Sound & editing

    Review Arabic pronunciation and pacing, assemble picture, voice and effects, and prepare the final video.
