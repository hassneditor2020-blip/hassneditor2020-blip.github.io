How to Choose a Video Editor in the UAE

A good choice is not based on one attractive video or the lowest price. The right editor understands the content goal, audience and platform, then turns the available material into a focused video through a clear workflow.

Updated 5 November 2026 · 3 min read · By Hassn Hassn — HASSN EDITOR
01

Start with relevant work

Review work close to your project—products, clinics, real estate, corporate content or AI video. Similarity in purpose matters more than matching colors alone.

02

Clarify the production scope

Decide whether you need editing of supplied footage or a complete path covering concept, script, filming, motion and sound. A clear scope prevents mismatched expectations and improves pricing accuracy.

03

Evaluate communication early

Clear responses, the right questions about audience, platform and deadline, and an organized review method are strong indicators of the working experience ahead.

04

Confirm delivery and revisions

Agree on formats, versions, revisions and deadline. Reels in 9:16, YouTube in 16:9 and paid ads may require different edits—not only resized files.

  • ✓ Request a quote based on the project scope
  • ✓ Choose an editor with relevant work
  • ✓ Confirm an organized review and delivery process
05

What to prepare before sending footage for editing

Share original files in one organized folder, together with the approved logo, fonts and colors. Specify the goal, platform and intended duration. Add a reference link and explain what you like about it. During review, combine team feedback into one list and use timecodes to identify each requested change.

  • ✓ Original files with clear names
  • ✓ Brand assets and a defined goal
  • ✓ Format, duration and deadline
  • ✓ Consolidated feedback with timecodes
06

Compare quotes for the same brief

For example, you need a 30-second Reel from existing footage. Give each editor the same source material, goal and reference. Ask whether the quote includes shot selection, editing, color, audio cleanup, titles, formats and review rounds. Custom motion and two delivery versions are a different scope from a simple single-format cut; compare clearly defined, equivalent deliverables.

  • ✓ Video duration, platform and format
  • ✓ Included work and client-supplied assets
  • ✓ Delivery versions and review rounds
  • ✓ First-draft and final-delivery dates
07

Ask how a relevant project was built

Look beyond transitions and color. In the published FM Restaurant video, pacing supports the live-event atmosphere; in Dental Motion 2026, animation explains a focused message; the product ad uses motion to present the offer. Ask about the editor’s actual role in the concept, filming, animation and sound, and why those decisions suited the intended viewer.

08

Agree on assets and usage before production

Clarify who supplies music, fonts, voice-over and images, and whether the video is intended for organic posts, paid ads or screens. Agree how files will be stored and whether project-file delivery or portfolio publication is included. These are practical scope and communication questions to settle with the editor before confirming the brief.
