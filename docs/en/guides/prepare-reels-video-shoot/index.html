How to Prepare a Reels Video Shoot for Your Business

Start a shoot by knowing what you want to say and what the viewer needs to see. Preparing products, the location, people and message in advance leaves more time for purposeful shots and helps produce a coherent, mobile-ready Reel.

Updated 7 November 2026 · 2 min read · By Hassn Hassn — HASSN EDITOR

Planning a finished video for your business? Review the production service, published projects and deliverables.

Videography and Reels by appointment
01

Define one message and one next step

Are you introducing a service, demonstrating a product or covering an event? Choose one main message and a relevant next step, such as an enquiry or a product-page visit. Avoid trying to explain every company service in one short video.

02

Turn the message into a shot list

An illustrative product-ad sequence could open with the product, show one feature in detail, demonstrate its use and finish with the brand and contact step. Adapt this plan to the product and goal; it is a planning example rather than a claim about a particular completed project.

  • ✓ An establishing shot of the location or product
  • ✓ Close-ups of the relevant details
  • ✓ Usage or presenter shots when needed
  • ✓ A suitable closing frame for the brand
03

Prepare the location, products and people

Arrange the background, clean the product and prepare any versions or accessories that will appear. Assign a person to coordinate access and participants, and choose a time suited to the light, sound and activity at the location. Share access details and filming restrictions before the appointment.

04

Review dialogue and sound before filming

For a presenter video, prepare concise, natural copy and review it with the speaker. Check background noise and decide whether the video needs live dialogue, later narration or on-screen text. This informs microphone, shot and pacing choices from the start.

05

Agree formats and versions

A vertical 9:16 Reel needs framing that keeps the product, face and copy clear of the app interface. Plan any 16:9 website or YouTube version before filming. Specify the video count, languages, captions and alternative openings in the brief.

06

Prepare brand assets and approval points

Share a suitable-quality logo, brand colors, product names, approved copy and contact details. Assign one person to consolidate feedback, and agree what needs approval before filming and what is reviewed during editing. The quote should define filming time, deliverables, revisions and schedule.

07

What should I send to arrange a shoot?

Send the goal, city, proposed appointment, video count, available assets and a relevant reference. Filming is available in Ajman, Sharjah and Dubai by prior appointment. Pricing follows scope review; editing and motion graphics can be completed remotely.
