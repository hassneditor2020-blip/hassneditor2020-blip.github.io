FAQ

Common questions before production

Can you produce HSE awareness videos?

I can edit approved safety content and supplied footage into induction or awareness videos, with motion graphics and captions. Your qualified HSE team approves all technical content before publication.

Can an industrial video be created from existing material?

Yes. The video can be built from supplied footage and technical files, with motion graphics or AI-assisted visuals added when they improve the explanation.

Does the factory need to be filmed?

That depends on the message and available assets. We can use client footage, coordinate suitable filming, or combine technical material and AI visuals within the agreed scope.

Which industrial video formats are available?

A 16:9 version can be prepared for presentations, websites and YouTube, with a 9:16 version for Reels and social platforms when required.
