FAQ

Questions before starting the project

How much does a Reels shoot cost?

The quote depends on filming time, location preparation, video count, editing, motion, sound and formats. Share your goal, city, versions and proposed appointment to agree the scope before booking.

Where is videography available?

Filming is available in Ajman, Sharjah and Dubai by prior appointment, after agreeing on the location, schedule and project scope.

Does the service include editing after the shoot?

Yes. Filming, editing, color grading, sound finishing and the required delivery formats can be handled through one workflow.

Can the video be prepared for Reels and YouTube?

Yes. The shoot is planned around the required formats, including vertical 9:16 for Reels and horizontal 16:9 for websites and YouTube.
