FAQ

Questions before starting the project

Is website and app design now your main service?

No. Videography, video editing and content production remain my core specialty. Web and app design is a complementary digital service for projects that need a more complete presence.

What kinds of websites can you build?

Business and service websites, landing pages, e-commerce experiences and digital platforms can be planned after defining the required functionality and the right technical approach.

Is the website designed for mobile too?

Yes. Mobile experience is treated as a core part of the design, including navigation, spacing, readability and performance checks before launch.

Does the service include SEO?

It includes core technical SEO preparation such as page structure, metadata, links and indexing readiness. Ongoing content and campaigns can be scoped separately.

Can I see real digital projects?

Yes. Syria Store is shown as an e-commerce project, and Kokab Fodool as an educational platform combining a website, Android app, games and learning content.
