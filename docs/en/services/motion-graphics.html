FAQ

Questions before starting the project

How much does a motion graphics video cost?

The quote depends on the copy, duration, scene count, animation complexity, sound, formats and review rounds. Share your idea, brand assets, a relevant reference and deadline so I can agree a clear scope before production.

What is the difference between video editing and motion graphics?

Editing arranges footage and sound into a video. Motion graphics adds purpose-built animation of text, graphics and elements to explain the idea. Both can be combined when a project needs real footage and a visual explanation.

Can motion graphics follow an existing brand identity?

Yes. The animation can be built from your logo, colors, type and available assets while preserving the brand style.

Can the project include voice-over?

A client-supplied voice-over can be integrated, or suitable voice production can be coordinated within the agreed scope.

Does motion graphics work for Reels?

Yes. Titles and movement can be designed for mobile viewing, including a vertical 9:16 format when required.
