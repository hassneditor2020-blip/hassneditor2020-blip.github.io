The project presents the fajita dish through movement, close detail and the journey to the table, helping viewers sense its heat and presentation. Filming was planned for a concise vertical format suited to the restaurant's social channels.

Food filming, angle selection, editing, color finishing and sound design work together while keeping the dish clear from the opening shot to the final frame.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

What the video needed to achieve

Turn the fajita presentation into an appealing visual moment that strengthens restaurant content and captures attention in Reels.

CREATIVE APPROACH

How the project was shaped

Filming focused on movement, heat and presentation, then arranged through concise pacing with color and sound that strengthen the dish experience.
