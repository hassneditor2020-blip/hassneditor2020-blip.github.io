Privacy policy

How the website handles your requests, choices and external services.

Project requests

This website does not require an account and does not store project-brief form entries on its server. The form opens a prepared WhatsApp message on your device. Review the message and choose whether to send it; opening the draft does not send it.

Analytics and your choices

Essential local storage remembers your privacy choice. Google Analytics loads only after you agree to analytics. You can refuse analytics and continue using the website, or change your choice with the Privacy settings button. When enabled, analytics records page visits, video interactions and contact-button clicks. Project-brief text and WhatsApp message contents are not included in these events.

Video and external platforms

Video covers may load from YouTube. On the homepage and portfolio listing, embedded players load when you choose to play. Dedicated project pages may load a YouTube player when the page opens. Those requests are handled by YouTube under its own privacy policy. Links to WhatsApp, Google and social platforms also lead to external services with their own policies.

Website assistant

If you send a message through the website assistant, the message is sent to the chat service to process your request. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in chat. WhatsApp is also available for discussing your project directly.

Contact

For questions about privacy, email hassneditor2020@gmail.com.
