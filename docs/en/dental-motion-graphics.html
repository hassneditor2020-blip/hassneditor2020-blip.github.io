FAQ

Common questions before production

What should I send for a clinic video?

Share the clinic branding, one message, approved copy, available images or diagrams, platform and deadline. The clinic reviews and approves medical information before it becomes scenes and titles.

Can it be produced without filming patients?

Yes. Motion graphics, illustrations and approved assets can explain the service visually using an approach appropriate to the message and brand.

How is dental video production priced?

Duration, scene count, graphics, animation, narration, captions, formats and revisions define the scope. Pricing follows a review of the approved copy and required assets.
